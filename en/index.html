FAQ

Do I need to provide my own market data?

Yes. FloorOne reads the M1 history of your MetaTrader 4 (every symbol of your broker), CSV files exported from MT4, MT5 or other platforms, and downloads Binance crypto data for free. The history grows by itself at every launch.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). No Mac version yet.

Are the prop firm rules accurate?

The Apex, Topstep and FTMO templates follow their main rules, and everything is editable. Prop firms change their terms regularly: always check them on their website before a real challenge.

Does it guarantee I'll pass my challenge?

No. FloorOne is a practice tool: it shows you honestly what your trades and your odds are worth. Simulated results do not guarantee future results.

Can I use it on several computers?

Yes, 2 PCs per license. To switch computers, release the license from the ? → License menu.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to activate the license and check it once a week. You can practice for up to 14 days offline.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime, in one click from your purchase email. You keep access until the end of the period already paid, with no further charge. The 7-day free trial lets you test everything before buying.
