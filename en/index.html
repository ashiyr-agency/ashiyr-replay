Frequently asked questions

Can I trade my real account with FloorOne?

Not yet. Today, live mode follows real crypto prices with a practice account: your orders are simulated, no real money. Connecting your own account, CFDs first then futures, at your broker or prop firm, is on the way.

Where does the market data come from?

The Symbols window downloads the 1-minute history of 110+ symbols for free (forex, indices, metals, energy, crypto). FloorOne also reads your broker platform's history and CSV files, and, as an option, tick data and futures.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). No Mac version yet.

Are the prop firm rules accurate?

The Apex, Topstep and FTMO templates follow their main rules, and everything can be edited. Prop firms change their conditions regularly: always check them on their website before a real challenge.

Does it guarantee I will win or pass my challenge?

No. FloorOne is an analysis, training and trade management platform: it shows you honestly what your trades and your odds are worth. Simulated or past results do not guarantee future results.

Can I use it on several computers?

Yes, 2 PCs per license. To switch computers, release the license from the ? → License menu.

Do I need an internet connection?

For live mode and downloads, yes. Replay, journal and analytics work offline: the license is checked once a week, and you can work up to 14 days without internet.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime, in one click from your purchase email. You keep access until the end of the period already paid, with no further charge. The 7-day free trial is there to test everything before you buy.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"

That's normal for recent software. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".
